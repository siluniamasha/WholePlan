A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What can I try in WholePlan?+

The prototype plans a concert ticket, a room and a return shuttle for one to six travelers. You can compare sample bundles and test seven different payment and recovery scenarios.

Will the demo charge real money?+

The simulator uses local payment records. PayPal Sandbox mode uses test accounts and test funds. The app shows which mode is active before you create a plan.

What happens if one purchase fails?+

The app stops the remaining checkout steps. You can start recovery to refund eligible captures and release uncaptured payment holds. Pending refunds and unresolved outcomes remain visible.

Where does AI help?+

When configured, Gemini interprets your request and extracts its components. You review the result. Your entered budget, supplier terms and payment checks are enforced by the application.

Are the hotels and tickets real listings?+

The supplier names, inventory, prices and cancellation policies are demonstration examples. Real supplier booking integrations are a future step.

Can every payment always be reversed?+

Recovery depends on the supplier’s terms and the payment provider’s status. Cancellation fees may remain. A refund can take time to become available through your bank.
